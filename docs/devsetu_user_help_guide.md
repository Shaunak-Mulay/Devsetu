# DEVSETU CONNECT
## User Help & Application Guide

Welcome to **DEVSETU CONNECT**.

DEVSETU CONNECT is a digital platform designed to help astrologers and spiritual-service partners conveniently manage Pooja services, bookings, payments, booking history, and communication with DEVSETU Administration.

---

# 1. GETTING STARTED

DEVSETU CONNECT has two main access areas:

### ASTROLOGER / USER

Astrologers can:

- Register an account
- Login using their Mobile Number and 6-Digit Login PIN
- View their profile
- Browse available Pooja services
- Book Pooja services
- Submit advance-payment information
- View booking status
- View booking history
- Receive notifications
- Contact DEVSETU Administration
- Create support requests
- Logout securely

### ADMIN

Authorized Administrators can:

- Manage astrologer accounts
- Validate registrations
- View user details
- Manage Pooja services
- View bookings
- Verify payments
- Confirm or reject bookings
- Manage booking status
- Manage support requests
- Manage notifications
- View platform information and reports

---

# 2. IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Before using DEVSETU CONNECT:

1. Provide correct and accurate information.
2. Keep your Login PIN confidential.
3. Never share your 6-Digit Login PIN with another person.
4. Check all client information before creating a booking.
5. Make payments only using the payment method displayed by DEVSETU.
6. Enter the correct UTR/Transaction ID after making payment.
7. Keep your payment receipt until your booking is confirmed.
8. Payment submission does not automatically mean that the booking is confirmed.
9. Booking confirmation is subject to payment verification and applicable Admin validation.
10. Do not provide your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, card PIN, banking password, or OTP to anyone.

---

# 3. REGISTRATION

To create an astrologer account:

1. Open DEVSETU CONNECT.
2. Select **Astrologer / User**.
3. Select **Register / Sign Up**.
4. Enter the requested information.
5. Enter your mobile number.
6. Enter your professional and location information.
7. Create a 6-Digit Login PIN.
8. Review your information.
9. Submit the registration.

After registration:

```text
Registration
      ↓
Account Created
      ↓
Unique Profile ID Generated
      ↓
Admin Validation
      ↓
Account Approved / Rejected
```

Your unique **Profile ID** is used to identify your DEVSETU account.

Keep your Profile ID safely for future communication with DEVSETU Support.

---

# 4. 6-DIGIT LOGIN PIN

DEVSETU currently uses a **6-Digit Login PIN** for astrologer authentication.

Your PIN must contain exactly six digits.

Example:

```text
● ● ● ● ● ●
```

### PIN Safety

* Do not share your PIN.
* Do not write your PIN where others can see it.
* Do not use an easily guessable PIN.
* Do not send your PIN through public or unsecured communication channels.

---

# 5. LOGIN

To login:

1. Select **Astrologer / User**.
2. Enter your registered Mobile Number.
3. Enter your 6-Digit Login PIN.
4. Select **Login**.

After successful authentication, you will be taken to the Astrologer Dashboard.

---

# 6. FORGOT LOGIN PIN

If you forget your 6-Digit Login PIN:

1. Open the Login screen.
2. Select **Forgot PIN**.
3. Follow the instructions displayed by the application.
4. A predefined request can be sent to DEVSETU Administration.

The request identifies the registered user and informs the Admin that the user has forgotten their Login PIN.

Example request:

> I am a registered DEVSETU user.
> Name: __________
> Profile ID: __________
> Mobile Number: __________
>
> I have forgotten my Login PIN.
> Please assist me with PIN recovery.

The Admin may verify your identity before assisting with PIN recovery.

**Never share your PIN publicly.**

# 12. ADVANCE PAYMENT

The current DEVSETU payment process uses **UPI / QR Code payment**.

The general process is:

```text
Create Booking
      ↓
View Advance Amount
      ↓
Display UPI QR
      ↓
Make Payment
      ↓
Enter UTR / Transaction ID
      ↓
Submit Payment Information
      ↓
Admin Verification
      ↓
Booking Confirmation
```

The applicable advance amount will be displayed by the application.

---

# 13. HOW TO MAKE PAYMENT

1. Open the payment section of your booking.
2. Check the booking amount.
3. Check the required advance amount.
4. Scan the DEVSETU UPI QR Code.
5. Complete the payment using your UPI application.
6. Save your payment receipt.
7. Return to DEVSETU.
8. Enter the required UTR / Transaction ID.
9. Submit the payment information.

### IMPORTANT

Do not submit a false UTR or transaction number.

Do not make another payment unless authorized DEVSETU Administration instructs you to do so.

---

# 14. PAYMENT VERIFICATION

After submitting your payment information:

```text
Payment Submitted
       ↓
Pending Admin Verification
       ↓
Admin Reviews Payment
       ↓
Payment Verified / Rejected
```

Payment submission does not automatically confirm the booking.

The Admin verifies the payment before the booking can be confirmed.

Keep your payment receipt until the payment and booking are successfully verified.

# 42. COMMON PROBLEMS

## I cannot login.

Check:

* Mobile Number
* 6-Digit Login PIN
* Account status
* Internet connection

If you have forgotten your PIN, select **Forgot PIN**.

---

## I forgot my PIN.

Select:

**Forgot PIN**

Follow the instructions and contact DEVSETU Administration for assistance.

---

## My booking is not confirmed.

Open **My Bookings** and check the current booking status.

If payment is still under verification, the booking may remain pending until Admin verification is completed.

If you need assistance, contact Support using your Booking ID.

---

## I made the payment but the booking is still pending.

Keep your payment receipt and UTR/Transaction ID.

Check the booking status.

If the payment remains pending after the expected verification period, contact DEVSETU Support.

Do not make another payment unless instructed by authorized DEVSETU Administration.

---

## My payment was rejected.

Contact DEVSETU Support with:

* Booking ID
* UTR / Transaction ID
* Payment receipt
* Relevant information

Do not submit false payment information.

---

## I cannot find my booking.

1. Make sure you are logged into the correct account.
2. Open **My Bookings**.
3. Check your booking history.
4. If the booking is still missing, contact Support with your Booking ID if available.

---

## Why can't I see another astrologer's information?

This is intentional.

DEVSETU uses privacy and role-based access controls so that astrologers can access their own account information rather than another astrologer's private information.
