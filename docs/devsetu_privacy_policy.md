# DEVSETU CONNECT – PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** 16 Aug 2026  
**Last Updated:** 16 Aug 2026  

DEVSETU CONNECT ("DEVSETU", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of users of the DEVSETU CONNECT mobile application and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

By creating an account or using DEVSETU CONNECT, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

---

### 1. About DEVSETU CONNECT

DEVSETU CONNECT is a platform designed to facilitate the management and booking of Pooja and spiritual services by registered astrologers and authorized DEVSETU administrators.

The application allows registered astrologers to:
- Create and manage their account
- Login using their registered mobile number and 6-digit PIN
- View their profile
- Browse available Pooja services
- Create bookings for clients
- Submit payment transaction information
- View booking status and booking history
- Receive service-related notifications
- Contact DEVSETU Administration
- Submit support requests

Authorized administrators use the platform to manage users, bookings, payment verification, services, support requests, notifications, and related operational activities.

---

### 2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide and operate DEVSETU CONNECT.

Depending on how you use the application, this may include the following:

#### 2.1 Account Information
When you register, we may collect:
- Full name
- Mobile number
- Email address, where provided
- City and state
- Location information provided by you
- Professional information
- Experience information
- DEVSETU Profile ID
- Login and authentication information

#### 2.2 Authentication Information
DEVSETU currently uses a 6-digit Login PIN for astrologer authentication.
The PIN is used to authenticate your account.
We apply appropriate security measures to protect authentication information. Your PIN should remain confidential and should not be shared with anyone.

#### 2.3 Booking Information
When you create a booking, we may collect information required to process the booking, including:
- Booking ID
- Selected Pooja or service
- Selected package
- Client/Yajmaan name
- Date of birth, where required for the selected service
- Mobile number
- Gotra, where provided
- Preferred date
- Performing city
- Venue/address
- Notes or special requirements
- Booking amount
- Advance-payment amount
- Booking status
- Payment status

You should provide only information that is necessary for the relevant booking.

#### 2.4 Payment Information
DEVSETU currently supports payment through the payment method displayed in the application, including UPI/QR-based advance payment where applicable.

We may collect:
- Booking ID
- Payment amount
- UTR/transaction ID
- Payment status
- Payment date/time
- Payment screenshot or supporting payment information, where the application provides such functionality

DEVSETU does not require your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, card PIN, banking password, or OTP.  
Never share these credentials with anyone claiming to represent DEVSETU.

#### 2.5 Support Information
When you contact DEVSETU Support, we may collect:
- Name
- Profile ID
- Booking ID
- Support category
- Subject
- Messages
- Attachments or supporting information, where supported
- Communication history

This information is used to investigate and respond to your request.

#### 2.6 Technical Information
When you use the application, certain technical information may be processed automatically, depending on the application's configuration and services used.

This may include:
- Device type
- Operating system
- Application version
- IP address
- Technical logs
- Error and diagnostic information
- Date and time of requests
- Network-related information

This information may be used to maintain security, troubleshoot errors, monitor reliability, and improve application performance.

### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for legitimate purposes related to operating DEVSETU CONNECT, including:

- **Account Management**: Creating and maintaining your account, authenticating your login, managing your profile, identifying your account, assisting with forgotten PIN requests.
- **Booking Management**: Creating bookings, generating Booking IDs, processing booking information, maintaining booking history, tracking booking status, coordinating requested services.
- **Payment Verification**: Recording payment information, associating payments with bookings, verifying UTR/transaction information, updating payment status, supporting booking confirmation, investigating payment-related issues.
- **Customer Support**: Receiving support requests, responding to queries, resolving account, booking and payment issues, maintaining support records.
- **Notifications**: Sending booking updates, sending payment updates, sending account-related notifications, sending support notifications, sending important service communications.
- **Security**: Protecting user accounts, detecting suspicious activity, preventing unauthorized access, investigating misuse, maintaining platform security.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may process information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations, lawful requests, court orders, or to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

### 4. How We Share Information

DEVSETU does not sell your personal information.

We may provide access to personal information when reasonably necessary to operate the platform. This may include sharing information with:
- **Authorized DEVSETU Personnel**: Personnel who validate registrations, process bookings, verify payments, provide support, manage the platform, and investigate security issues.
- **Technology and Service Providers**: Third-party providers for cloud database infrastructure, cloud hosting, email delivery, SMS/notification services, push notifications, and security services.
- **Legal Authorities**: Disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law or valid legal process.

### 5. Privacy Between Users

DEVSETU uses role-based access controls.

An astrologer is intended to have access to information relevant to their own account and activities, including:
- Their profile
- Their bookings
- Their booking history
- Their notifications
- Their support requests

An astrologer should not have access to another astrologer's private account, booking, or support information.

Authorized administrators may have broader access necessary to operate and manage the platform.
